This critical feedback forced a strategic pivot. The AI abandoned the load everything at once approach and adopted a meticulous, book-by-book methodology as requested by the user. The final, successful phase focused exclusively on perfecting the KJV version of Genesis. This involved extensive web research to cross-reference the correct text and structure, leading to a hybrid parser () that used web-verified content. This finally produced a clean, accurate, and complete version of Genesis, setting the standard for all future data loading tasks. The core lesson was that the source data's complexity required a more precise, validated, and incremental approach rather than a brute-force one.

**Problem Statement:** The current database is critically flawed. Initial data loading attempts from user-provided text files resulted in severe data corruption. This makes the application unusable for its intended purpose.

**Current Mandate:** The development process has been reset to a meticulous, book-by-book approach to ensure data integrity. The immediate focus is on the KJV 1611 version. The task is to:
1.  Isolate and perfect the data for a single book at a time, starting with Genesis.
2.  Ensure each book has all its chapters and verses, with content that is 100% accurate and verified against authoritative online sources.
3.  Completely eliminate the cross-contamination of text between books.
4.  Once one book is perfect, proceed to the next until all 80 books are correctly loaded for both versions.
</product_requirements>
