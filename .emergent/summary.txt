A user authentication system (registration, login, logout) was added for personalized progress, with guest access prompting sign-in.

The product vision has significantly expanded to a Comprehensive Biblical Study Suite. This now requires integrating:

2.  **Precept Upon Precept Guide**: A system (inspired by ) that connects scripture to scripture, precept upon precept, line upon line, here a little, there a little. This includes 613 Precept Navigator (connecting laws to verses) and Living Precept Chains (thematic verse connections).
3.  **Concordances**: For word studies.
4.  **Lexicons**: For original language research (Hebrew/Greek).

6.  **Community Features**: Discussion forums, study groups, shared note-taking.

*   **Free Tier**: First 100 laws, Genesis/Exodus, basic search, 5 daily quiz questions, limited precept connections, guest progress, daily mitzvah preview.

*   **Scholar Subscription (.99/month)**: Everything in Premium, plus Hebrew/Greek lexicons, audio narration, advanced study notes, multiple Bible translations, Rabbinic commentary, Jewish calendar integration, personalized study plans, enhanced search.
